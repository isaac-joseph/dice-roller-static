How to play

Dice roller is played with five six-sided dice. On your turn you may roll all five dice up to three times, keeping any dice you like between rolls, as you try to build the best-scoring combination. This app handles only the rolling: press Roll the dice (or hit Enter) to roll all five dice and see fresh results and their total above. A set of dice is rolled for you automatically as soon as the page opens.
